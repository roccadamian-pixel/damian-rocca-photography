Family Sessions

Real families, real light.

Kids change fast — a look, a laugh, a way of holding someone's hand that won't last another year. A family session is a chance to hold onto exactly where you are right now, without the fuss of a formal shoot. We meet somewhere with good natural light — a beach, a park, a patch of bush, or your own backyard — and spend our time together letting the day unfold rather than staging it.

Siblings playing, candid movement
Who it suits

Families who want it real, not rigid.

Young families, growing families, families who've never done this before and aren't sure they'll enjoy it. If your kids won't sit still for a formal portrait, that's exactly the energy this style is built around.

What to wear

Coordinated, not matched.

Soft, muted tones work best against natural light and won't fight with the background — think sand, sage, dusty blue, soft white. Steer clear of big logos, busy patterns, or anything neon; it pulls the eye away from faces.

Textures photograph beautifully — a linen shirt, a knit, a flowy dress. Everyone doesn't need to wear the same colour, just colours that sit comfortably together. Comfortable shoes matter more than you'd think, and if your little one has a favourite toy or blanket, bring it along — the real stuff is what makes these photos yours.

Common questions

What if it rains?

Weather-related rescheduling is always free and won't cost you your deposit.

Can we shoot at home instead?

Of course — some families feel most like themselves in their own space, and that comes through in the photos.

How far ahead should we book?

A few weeks is usually plenty, though it's worth booking earlier around Christmas and Mother's Day.
